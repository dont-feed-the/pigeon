decisions to implementation and verification entry points; a file's existence
is not a passing result or proof of complete conformance. Actual run results
and their source commits belong to the milestone reports. This inventory
does not close M6, the complete trace audit, or M7's elapsed gates.

The inventory started against `cf7447c`; the logging row includes the
accompanying implementation follow-up. Proposed, withdrawn, rejected
and superseded records remain in the [ADR index](adr/README.md); they are
not silently promoted to accepted decisions here. Later changes must keep
this table and the recorded limitations current.

## Remaining release gates

M6 still needs the measurements and native scheduling work recorded in
[its report](reports/resources-7.md). The filesystem checkpoint also separates
real ext4/btrfs/xfs evidence from unmeasured ZFS and Mac runtime combinations.

M7 still needs the complete law/trace audit, exact-head refinement validation,
joining/signing security review and every new-design v0.N upgrade matrix.
The [join lifetime review](reports/join-deadline-security-7.md) reproduces
and bounds a silent admitter; it records the remaining review findings.
The [backup cleanup review](reports/backup-join-preservation-security-7.md)
reproduces and preserves files added during a failed backup join.
It also requires seven actual green nights, a continuous 72-hour soak, and
two weeks of release-candidate dogfood. No fixture dates, repeated quick runs
or prior M3 dogfood substitute for those elapsed intervals.

The [v1.1 scope](roadmap.md#after-v10) remains native Windows, the status
icon, Mac/NAS backups, phone viewers, the placement planner and per-file
freeing. Their names in accepted ADRs do not make them implemented.
