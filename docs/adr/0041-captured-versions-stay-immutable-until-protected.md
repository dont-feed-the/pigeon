# 0041. A captured version stays immutable on its laptop until it is protected; thin only after

- **Status:** Withdrawn, 14 October 2026, by the agent, at Guillaume's request
  (chat): moved to the specification.

This record held a rule that the specification now carries: [07 §7.8](../spec/07-capture-and-install.md) and [03 §3.4](../spec/03-repository-and-roles.md).
Under the [policy](README.md#when-to-write-an-adr) it is not an ADR: it is
a safety invariant (P1, P2) whose alternative loses unprotected versions (E18). Its full text, with its context, evidence and
alternatives, is at [commit 1a6067e](https://github.com/mariogeiger/pigeon/blob/1a6067e/docs/adr/0041-captured-versions-stay-immutable-until-protected.md).
