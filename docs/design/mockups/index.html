What Martine sees

Martine is not technical. Every page answers, in order: are my files safe? If not, why, and what happens next? Must I do something? (usually not).

The four words

Only on this computer Shared, not safe yet Safe Not checked since Monday 18:02

Always icon + word + colour. Internally, and in --json: local, published, protected, uncertain.
